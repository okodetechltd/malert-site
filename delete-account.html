Delete your MAlert account

In the app

  1. Open MAlert and sign in.
  2. Women and riders: go to your Profile and tap Delete account. Facility staff, committee members and managers: tap the account icon at the top, or the link above Sign out in the side menu.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

An account cannot be deleted while it has an emergency in progress. Finish or cancel the emergency first.

Without the app

Email okodetechltd@gmail.com from any address, with the phone number you signed up with. We will confirm by SMS to that number and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your login, profile, pregnancy records held by facilities, rider documents and voice notes are removed permanently.

What is kept

Records of past emergency trips and payments are kept for as long as they are needed for safety, financial and reporting purposes, or as the law requires. See the privacy policy.
