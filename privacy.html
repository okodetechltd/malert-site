MAlert Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

MAlert connects pregnant women in emergencies with nearby riders and health facilities. It is operated by Okode Technology ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and phone number. Phone numbers are used to sign in by SMS code.
  • Location: your precise location while the app is open: a woman's, to send the nearest rider; a rider's, to dispatch and guide them. We do not collect location in the background.
  • Pregnancy and health details (women): due date, the facility you attend, visits and check-ins recorded by that facility, and a relative's contact number for emergencies.
  • Voice notes: a short recording describing an emergency, if you choose to record one.
  • Rider documents: Ghana Card, driving licence and vehicle details, to approve riders.
  • Payments: trip amounts, payment status and screenshots of payment receipts. Mobile money and card payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers or PINs.
  • Device data: a notification token so we can send you alerts.

How we use it

Only to run the service: dispatching riders, alerting facilities, tracking a trip until arrival, paying riders, letting facilities follow up their patients, and keeping the service safe. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps.

Who can see it

  • During an emergency, the rider and facility handling it see the woman's name, phone number, location and voice note.
  • A woman sees her rider's name, phone number and vehicle.
  • Facility staff see the records of their own patients.
  • Zone managers and MAlert administrators see what they need to approve riders and oversee trips.
  • Service providers who process data for us: Google Firebase (hosting, sign-in, database, storage, notifications), Google Maps, our SMS provider and our payment provider.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Records of emergency trips and payments are kept after deletion for as long as they are needed for safety, financial and reporting purposes, or as the law requires.

Deleting your account

In the app, open your profile or account menu and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your login, profile, documents and voice notes. You can also request deletion here.

Security

Data is stored with Google Firebase and protected by access rules that limit each person to what their role needs. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

MAlert is intended for adults. If you believe a child's data was provided to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time, including under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Contact

Changes
