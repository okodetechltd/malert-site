How it works

From the first sign of trouble to the delivery room.

Every minute counts in an obstetric emergency. MAlert takes the searching, calling and waiting out of getting there.

She taps SOS

She chooses the facility she wants, can record a short voice note saying what's wrong, and her location is shared automatically.

The nearest rider comes

Approved riders nearby are alerted at once. The first to accept is guided to her, and she watches the rider arrive on the map.

The facility is ready

The facility gets her details as soon as she's on her way and confirms it's ready. If it has no bed, it sends her straight to another one.

Who it's for

One app, everyone who gets her there.

Pregnant women

  • Call for help with one tap, even when you can't explain on the phone
  • See your rider coming and share the trip with family
  • Get health tips from your own facility, read aloud

Riders

  • Emergency alerts that ring like a call, so none is missed
  • The route to her, then on to the facility, on the map
  • Get paid for every completed trip, with your earnings in one place

Health facilities

  • Know who is coming, how far away she is, and what's wrong
  • Mark ready, or redirect her to another facility when you're full
  • Follow up your patients with visits, check-ins and audio tips

Communities and zone managers

  • Chango committees cover rides for the women they enrol
  • Zone managers approve riders and step in when no one accepts
  • Clear records of every trip and payment
Features

Built for real emergencies, on real roads.

Live tracking

She sees her rider approach, and family can follow the trip from a link in any browser.

Voice notes

A few seconds of audio tells the rider and facility what to expect, without typing.

Alerts that can't be missed

Riders' phones ring for an emergency, and facilities hear "she has arrived" read aloud.

Facility redirect

A full facility sends her to the nearest open one in a tap, and her rider's route updates.

Flexible payment

Mobile money, cash, or a Chango committee fund, settled after she's safely there.

Her language

Spoken guidance in English, Twi and Dagbanli for women who prefer to listen.
